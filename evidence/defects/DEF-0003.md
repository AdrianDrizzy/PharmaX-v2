## Expected

PHX-SRS-001 names these forms normatively and §1.6 states the convention
explicitly:

> *"Windows Forms are named `frmXxx` throughout and **the name in this document
> is the name in the solution**."*

§13.6 is titled *"Monitoring — frmSyncMonitor"*. Appendix E states every control
variable is *"changed only by an audited edit in **frmControlVariables**"*.

## Actual

46 forms exist on disk. 31 form names appear in the spec (excluding the `frmXxx`
convention placeholder). **18 spec-named forms do not exist.** Of those, 7 are
renames where the capability demonstrably exists, and **11 have no equivalent**.

Full reconciliation: [`../00-preflight/screen-inventory.csv`](../00-preflight/screen-inventory.csv)

## Impact on the wave plan

Five waves have exit criteria that name a capability in group B:

| Wave | Exit criterion at risk | Blocking absence |
|---|---|---|
| **W1** | *"Full role × permission grid executed; TC-12 audit-immutability confirmed"* | `frmRolePermissions`, `frmAuditQuery` |
| **W5** | *"TC-05, TC-09 executed"* | `frmRecall` (TC-09 is batch recall across eleven patients) |
| **W6** | *"Three-way match tested including mismatch cases"* | `frmInvoiceMatch` |
| **W7** | *"TC-14 executed; every journal balanced"* | `frmPeriodClose` |
| **W9** | *"TC-02, TC-03, TC-07, TC-08 executed"* | `frmSyncMonitor` |

Additionally, **Section E axis 15 (audit conformance) is specified as applying to
every case in the engagement** and depends on `frmAuditQuery`. With no audit
query surface, the third assertion layer of §L.6 (*"UI state, database state,
audit log — all three must agree"*) can only be reached by direct database
query, never through the product. That is workable for evidence but means
TC-12 (*audit log cannot be edited or cleared, and the attempt is itself
audited*) has no user-facing path to exercise.

## Confidence

This is a **static** reconciliation and is stated provisionally. A capability can
exist under a name the keyword probe did not guess, or be reached by a keyboard
shortcut rather than a menu item. The eleven group-B entries were probed three
ways (source keyword, `frmMain` menu tree, `MainPresenter` routing table), but
only driving the live application settles it.

Two entries are flagged as likely over-calls and should be re-tested first:

- **`frmReceipt`** — `PharmaX.Adapters/Printing/ReceiptDocument.cs` exists and a
  receipt is a printed document, not necessarily a form. Likely **not** a defect.
- **`frmPriceChange`** / **`frmExpiryDashboard`** — both have menu entries
  (*Price Changes*, *Expired Stock*) whose scope could not be read from source.

`frmControlVariables` is the opposite case: it is the **best-evidenced** entry in
the list, because the read-only routing is visible directly at
`MainPresenter.cs:327` and Appendix E's wording (*"an audited edit"*) is
unambiguous.

Severity is set at 2 on the §20.3 limb *"a parity capability is unavailable"*,
and will be re-assessed per form once the app runs.
